## Project page update

**Q-Day Drill: the bot has our private key. It still cannot move the money.**

Today is the Q-Day Drill. The fest handed a bot the ECDSA private key of our testnet account and told it to take the funds: native transfers, open an EVM window, EVM transfers, approvals, drain the contract.

This is the exact attack Seedproof is built for. On QoreChain the private key is only half of an account. Every transaction also needs a post-quantum ML-DSA-87 signature, and on our account that key lives on a separate Home Key device, not in the 24 words.

Where we stand with the drill underway:

- **Our proof transaction landed.** A normal send from the drill account, signed by the Home Key, went through at 07:12 UTC. Code 0. Hash `775AC44B…398008`.
- **The bot has no lane to spend through.** We left no EVM window open, so even holding the private key there is nothing for it to move funds on.
- **The savings sit behind the vault.** Any withdrawal needs a Home Key signature checked on chain by `pqcVerify`, and still waits out a delay that a guardian can cancel.
- **The account so far: untouched.** About 27 QOR on the account, and its only transaction today is our own proof. Thief attempts are refused before they reach a block, so they cost the account nothing.

The drill runs until 23:59 UTC tonight. We will post the final tally, attacks attempted and value kept, once it closes.
