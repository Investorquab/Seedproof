A standard account's post-quantum key is made from the same 24 words. The QoreX docs say it plainly: the key needs no separate backup. Accounts made from a raw private key with SDK 0.12 or earlier made it from that key instead.

Two devices. Neither holds both keys.

Pocket

Your phone. It keeps the classical key from your 24 words and spends freely inside today's window: a few payments, to the token you chose, until tonight.

  • Shows what a thief could take right now
  • Asks the Home Key for anything bigger
  • Never holds the post-quantum key

Home Key

A second device that stays home, even an old phone with no network. It holds a post-quantum key that no seed phrase can rebuild.

  • Reads every request back to you in plain words
  • Refuses anything it cannot read
  • Can be rebuilt by 2 of your 3 guardians if lost

People lose their words, not their keys

On Christmas Eve 2025 a tampered Trust Wallet browser extension shipped a backdoor that sent users' recovery phrases to an attacker. Infostealers now search hundreds of wallet extensions for them. And in 2025 CertiK counted 72 attacks where someone was physically forced to open their wallet.

Each of these takes the 24 words. On QoreChain today, the 24 words are both keys. Seedproof makes them one.
